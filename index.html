    About

    I am a Level 3, Term 2 student in the Bachelor of Urban and Regional Planning (BURP) programme at Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET), Bangladesh.

    My research sits where geospatial science meets planning practice. I combine satellite remote sensing, spatial statistics and multi-criteria weighting methods such as PCA, Shannon entropy and AHP to build composite risk indices, assess flood, drought and waterlogging hazard, and simulate urban growth and land-use change across Bangladesh.

    I am open to research collaborations, assistantships and graduate study opportunities in GIS, remote sensing, disaster risk reduction and climate-resilient planning.
